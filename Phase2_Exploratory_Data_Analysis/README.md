## Project Overview

This repository contains **Phase 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of the **NexaTel Customer Churn Analytics** project completed as part of the **Internmo Data Analyst Internship**.

The objective of this phase is to explore customer churn patterns, identify important relationships within the customer base, and understand potential churn drivers across customer, billing, geographic, service, network, and retention dimensions.

The analysis uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to transform the cleaned NexaTel dataset into meaningful business insights.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this phase are to:

- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Compare churn across different billing cycles.
- Identify churn patterns across customer tenure groups.
- Examine the relationship between unresolved complaints and churn.
- Compare churn across customer segments.
- Identify geographic areas with higher numbers of churned customers.
- Compare average data usage across city tiers.
- Analyze the distribution of monthly customer charges.
- Examine network quality in relation to customer churn.
- Analyze retention campaign responses and retained customer share.
